Archer TD Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Archer TD ("the app") is a single-player offline game made by AngryDoc. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

We collect no data

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and it makes no network connections.

Data on your device

Your game progress (stage, coins and upgrades) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device. You can erase it with "Reset save" in the game or by deleting the app.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example by adding in-app purchases through Apple), we will update this page and the App Store privacy details first.
